# Info

60 ultra-wide banners for rules / about / announcement headers.

## What they are

Cinematic **3:1-ish** plates. Recurring crafts:

- Night ruin / game-illustration atmosphere, teal-blue, particles
- Luxury gold: dotted field, rim light from bottom corners, empty stage
- Gold-and-charcoal fracture matching companion lines
- Rounded-rect “tablets” with 3 dots on the top edge and lanterns on the right
- Photographic or painterly wide crops with a quiet middle

Type is rare. The banner is a backdrop; Discord text or a second overlay carries the rules.

Observed sizes include 1500×500, 2400×514, 3264×1020, 4096×1365, 1080×370.

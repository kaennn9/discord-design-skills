# Info — design rules

1. Ratio ~3:1. Recommended export 1800×600 or 2400×800 PNG.
2. Quiet center. All fireworks live in the corners and short edges.
3. Optional 3-dot chrome on the top edge of tablet-style infos.
4. Rounded corners (~24–40px) if the piece is a card, not a full-bleed landscape.
5. Same motif as the pack line (gold shards on info ⇒ gold shards on line).
6. Overlay text in Discord should sit at 4.5:1 contrast on the center.
7. No paragraphs baked into the PNG unless the brief is a poster; baked text becomes mush at chat width.
8. Original scenery and light. Do not copy ruin or gold plates from this folder.

# Discord Classic

31 files in two subfolders: `info/` (13) and `packs/` (18).

## info/

Ultra-wide atmospheric plates: smoke, silk, greyscale veils. Little or no type. These are **stages** for overlay text in `#info` / rules channels. Recurring sizes 1500×500, 2400×800.

## packs/

Mix of:

- Pale geometric banners with folded violet corner ribbons and a quiet grey field (room for a title)
- Circular abstract logos (teal ribbon knot, 512-class)
- Extra wide decorative plates

The “classic” look is restrained: muted palette, no character art, no loud neon, identity through folds and smoke.

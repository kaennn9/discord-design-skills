# Lines — design rules

1. Height 50–120px. Width 800–1440px. It must still read as a line.
2. Transparent PNG or looping GIF. No solid white strip.
3. Same motif as the pack info corners.
4. No readable words.
5. GIF: loop, limited palette, keep under 2MB if possible.
6. End-weighted or continuous — pick one.
7. Check at 550px chat width; if the middle vanishes, thicken the bar slightly, not the ornaments.
8. Original ornaments. Do not trace GIFs from this folder.

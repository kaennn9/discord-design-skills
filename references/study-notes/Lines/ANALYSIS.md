# Lines

116 files. Decorative **dividers** posted under embeds and headers.

## What they are

Very wide, very short. Families:

- Still PNG ~1439×90 or 1006×72 — end-weighted ornaments, gold fracture, purple smoke bars
- GIF ~800×80 — sparkle, pulse, pixel rain, looping glow
- Tiny JPEG/PNG bars (some under 2KB) — too crude; do not emulate the muddy ones

A good line is a crop of the pack’s edge language. Gold-shard info banners in this library have matching gold-shard lines.

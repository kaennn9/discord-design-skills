# Packs

202 files in **39 groups**. A group shares the Discord message-id prefix before the first underscore. Typical group size is 4–6 assets; a few kits go to 9–10.

## What these images are

Complete (or partial) **server graphic kits** sold or shared as one drop: banner, logo, welcome, info, line, ticket, giveaway, music, system/bot, verify.

Filename roles seen: `banner`, `logo`, `welcome`/`WLC`, `info`, `line`, `ticket`, `giveaway`, `music`, `system`, `bot`, `prove`/`protiction`, `avatar`.

## Visual families sampled

- **Quiet Ramadan** — navy void, hanging lanterns, mosque silhouette, empty center (`RAMADAN_*`).
- **Smoke black** — charcoal veils, 3 dots on the top edge, lanterns on the info right, circle ticket icon with glow.
- **Gold fracture** — charcoal field, torn gold shards on corners; the line is a crop of the same shards.
- **Geometric / ribbon welcome** — avatar circle + colored tablet, often purple/teal.
- **Function icons** — 512 circles: ticket stub, gears, carts.

Most banners are 16:9 or ultra-wide with **quiet centers** so Discord bots can overlay text. Logos cluster at **512×512**.

## What a pack means here

Not a random ZIP of pretty pictures. One DNA: palette, motif, chrome (3-dots, rims), light. If the welcome is neon purple and the info is gold lanterns, it is two packs, not one.

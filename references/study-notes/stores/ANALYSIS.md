# stores

## What they are

Shop brands, not communities. Hero is a logo on a dark grid or device screen (`Nuit Store`). Icon is a lettermark. Copy is the store name, not `… Server`.

Dark metal skin dominates this folder. Identity is colder, more system, less character illustration.

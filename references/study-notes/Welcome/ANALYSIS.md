# Welcome

51 files. In-channel **welcome** graphics, mostly with a **left circle** where a bot pastes the new member’s avatar.

## Anatomy

- Transparent or pure black outside the shape (Discord dark theme)
- Circle + rim on the left
- Flowing body (ribbon, cloud, tablet, leaf) in pack color
- Optional `WELCOME TO` / Arabic أهلاً to the right of the circle
- Must not cover the hole

Examples in this folder: purple ribbon welcome, cyan circle on forest cloud, olive tablet with dotted arc, full-bleed illustrated welcomes (weaker — they fight the avatar).

Sizes vary (1280×720, 756×430, square). Strongest pieces are landscape with a huge empty circle and no extra background box.

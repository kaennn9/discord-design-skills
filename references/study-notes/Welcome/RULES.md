# Welcome — design rules

1. Transparent PNG. No white or grey canvas.
2. Left circle diameter ≈ 34–40% of canvas height, rim 4–8px in accent color.
3. Keep the hole empty. No texture, no logo inside the circle.
4. Wordmark to the right, small. One language or AR+EN, not a paragraph.
5. Body shape shares pack motif and palette.
6. Export 1280×720 (or 1100×500). Check at 550px width.
7. Do not use a full landscape illustration unless the avatar sits in a clearly designed frame.
8. Original ribbons and tablets only.

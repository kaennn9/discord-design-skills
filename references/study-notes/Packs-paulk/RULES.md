# Packs-paulk — design rules

1. One board, 1920×1080 (or 16:9). This is packaging, not a Discord upload.
2. Show: icon, banner, 2–3 headers, 3 round icons. Optional phone invite.
3. Background = identity wash, not a screenshot of Discord.
4. Credit and invite URL once, small, in the footer.
5. Align to a 12-column or simple modular grid. Same corner radius on every card.
6. Do not put different servers on one sheet.
7. Glyph icons must be cut from the same material as the banner.
8. Arabic and Latin wordmarks share optical weight; do not fake-bold Arabic.
9. Original identity only — do not recreate ALPHA / NRS / Sorn marks.
